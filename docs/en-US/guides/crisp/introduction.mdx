# Product Introduction

A smart assistant that comprehensively boosts the efficiency of your customer communication.

The Crisp AI Plugin is a cloud service provided by Haruka Network, designed to deliver an efficient communication experience for users.

It supports a wide range of features and is suitable for both *individual users* and *teams*.

## Key Features

- **Personalized Settings**: Customize the reply avatar and name to enhance your brand image.
- **Collaborative Project Management**: Multiple members can collaborate on a project, improving team productivity.
- **Smart / Manual Reply Switching**: Smart replies are automatically disabled after a manual reply, ensuring customers receive quality service.
- **Smart Keyword Matching**: Trigger logic based on custom keywords and regular expressions to respond precisely to customer needs.
- **OpenAI-Powered Smart Replies**: Leverage advanced AI technology to deliver an intelligent interactive experience.
- **Option Replies**: Combine with keywords to offer diverse reply options that meet different customer needs.
- **Telegram Notifications**: Push messages to a Telegram topic group in real time, with each chat session displayed in its own topic.
- **Telegram Replies**: Reply with text and images directly from Telegram, delivered to customers in real time.

The Crisp AI Plugin provides you with a comprehensive smart communication solution to help grow your business.
